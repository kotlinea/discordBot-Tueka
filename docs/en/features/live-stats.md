# Live statistics channels

Choose a display voice channel and a name template to show member counts and other supported values. The Bot needs Manage Channels. Discord rate limits mean updates are not instantaneous. An empty template uses the default.

Updates after member joins, departures, and role creation or deletion are grouped every five seconds. Counts are also refreshed every five minutes.

Disabling the feature preserves the channel and its last name.
